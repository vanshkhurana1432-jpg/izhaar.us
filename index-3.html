About Izhaar

A community built on presence, not advice

Izhaar — meaning expression in Urdu and Hindi — was born from a simple belief: that every person deserves to be heard without fear or judgement.

We are an NGO-run peer support platform connecting people who are struggling with trained volunteer listeners. No therapy. No advice. Just a safe, anonymous space to feel less alone.

Privacy & Safety

Both parties are always anonymous

We built trust into every layer of Izhaar.

🎭

You are anonymous

No name, email or phone number ever collected. You receive a session code — nothing more.

🛡

Listeners are too

Peer Listeners also use anonymous IDs. No personal data is exchanged between either party.

🔒

Encrypted & unrecorded

All chats are end-to-end encrypted. Voice and video calls are never recorded or stored.

🌱

Volunteer-led

Every listener is a trained volunteer who has chosen to give their time out of genuine care.

🩹

Not therapy

We are not a crisis service or substitute for professional help — but we are always here to listen.

💚

Free, always

No subscriptions, no payments, no in-app purchases. Izhaar is a gift to anyone who needs it.

Be part of Izhaar

Be a part of something meaningful

You don't need all the answers. You don't need to fix anyone. Just showing up with an open heart is enough — and we'll walk alongside you every step of the way.

Peer Listener · Volunteer 🌱
Lend your presence, not just your words
Becoming a Peer Listener at Izhaar means offering your time and genuine attention to someone who needs to feel heard. We provide full training, a supportive community, and everything you need to show up with confidence and care.
  • ✓No experience needed — just willingness to listen
  • ✓Full training and ongoing support provided
  • ✓Flexible hours — show up when you can
  • ✓Join a warm, compassionate community
  • ✓Both you and those you support stay anonymous

All volunteer applications are processed securely. Your contact details are visible only to our admin team and never displayed publicly.
